Step-by-Step Schedule

Tuesday, Sep 15
1
1:00 PM – 1:40 PM Metro Transit (Line E → Line D)

Depart Oporto Fly Hotel to Porto São Bento

Walk from Oporto Fly Hotel to Verdes Station (or Aeroporto Station). Board Metro Line E (Purple Line) towards Estádio do Dragão. Transfer at Trindade Station to Line D (Yellow Line), alighting 2 stops later at São Bento Station (~1:35 PM). Take 5 minutes to admire the famous hand-painted azulejo tiles inside the central station hall.

São Bento Station Azulejo Hall
Transit Tip

Buy a Z4 Andante ticket (€2.30) before boarding and validate before entering platforms.

Total Transit Time: ~35–40 mins
2
1:40 PM – 2:40 PM Lunch & Dining

Lunch at Time Out Market Porto (or local spots)

Head inside Time Out Market Porto, built directly into the refurbished South Wing of São Bento Station (or choose a traditional eatery nearby along Rua das Flores). Choose from top local chefs serving Francesinhas, fresh codfish, octopus, bifanas, and Douro wines.

Time Out Market Dining Hall
Popular Choices
  • Francesinha (Porto signature sandwich)
  • Bacalhau à Brás (shredded codfish)
  • Artisanal pastries & espresso

Estimated cost: ~€12–€20 per person

3
2:40 PM – 4:30 PM Sightseeing & Photo Loop

Historic Landmarks Walking Loop

Walk uphill from São Bento along a continuous, logical sightseeing loop:

1st Stop

Torre e Igreja dos Clérigos

Baroque church & 75m bell tower.

Photo Stop

Livraria Lello

Famous bookstore facade photo stop outside.

3rd Stop

Igreja do Carmo & Carmelitas

Twin churches with famous blue tile mural wall.

4th Stop

Praça da Liberdade & Aliados

Grand central square & avenue to City Hall.

4
4:30 PM – 5:45 PM Local Markets & Delis

Mercado do Bolhão & Traditional Gourmet Delis

Walk ~8 mins to Porto's culinary center. Explore the two-story Mercado do Bolhão, then step right outside onto Rua Formosa to browse two historic gourmet shops: A Pérola do Bolhão (Art Nouveau tile facade) and Comer e Chorar por Mais.

Historic Fresh Market Mercado do Bolhão
Rua Formosa Delis A Pérola do Bolhão & Comer e Chorar por Mais
🛍️ Regional Specialties: Queijo da Serra da Estrela (soft mountain sheep cheese), Presunto (cured ham), gourmet canned sardines (conservas), and bottled Port wine.
5
5:45 PM – 6:15 PM Shopping Mall Stop (~30 Mins)

ViaCatarina Shopping

Turn onto Rua de Santa Catarina (Porto’s bustling pedestrian retail boulevard). Spend ~30 minutes browsing fashion stores or picking up personal items.

Architecture Highlight: Visit the top-floor food court styled after traditional 19th-century northern Portuguese village houses!

6
6:15 PM – 7:30 PM Evening Dinner

Dinner on Rua de Santa Catarina

Relax and enjoy dinner at a sit-down restaurant along Santa Catarina or at the themed ViaCatarina dining court before heading back.

7
7:30 PM Onward Direct Metro Return

Return Metro to Hotel (Post Rush-Hour)

Walk 3 minutes to Bolhão Metro Station. Board Line E (Purple Line) heading directly toward Aeroporto, riding smoothly back to Verdes Station near your hotel.

Metro Zone Info

A single journey between Verdes/Airport and central Porto requires a Z4 ticket (€2.30). Validate your Andante card before entering platforms.

Tipping & Payments

Tipping is optional. Rounding up to the nearest euro or leaving ~5% for good service is plenty. Credit cards are widely accepted.

Language & Courtesy

English is widely spoken! Simple greetings like "Olá" (Hello) and "Obrigado/a" (Thank you) are always appreciated by locals.
